About Evelyn Pulido

My Approach

I am a Software Engineer and UX/UI Designer passionate about creating accessible, intuitive, and visually memorable digital products. My work combines the logical thinking of development with the empathy of user-centered design.

I firmly believe that every great digital experience begins by listening to people, iterating solutions through real prototypes, and refining code to ensure performance, accessibility, and inclusion across every screen.

My Pillars

  • Human-Centered Design
  • Accessibility & Inclusion (WCAG AA)
  • Clean & Scalable Code
  • Artificial Intelligence Integration
